Why it is free

Made as ṣadaqah jāriyah

No adverts, no account, no tracking. Everything you do stays on your phone. If it helps you remember Allah even once, that is the reward hoped for. Please remember the makers, and everyone whose work went into its sources, in your duʿāʾ.

Verified sourcesArabic from Ḥiṣn al-Muslim and the Qurʾān; translations verbatim from sunnah.com and The Clear Quran; every hadith with its grade.
The Sunnah firstCount on your fingers, at the times the Sunnah gives, with the numbers it sets.
PrivateNo account and no analytics. The internet is used only for recitations you choose to download.
Open sourceThe code is public, so anyone can check it, learn from it and help improve it.
