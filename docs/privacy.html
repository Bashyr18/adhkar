Adhkār Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Adhkār has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, store or share any personal data.

Data on your device

Your counts, prayer log, journal, settings and location stay on your device (phone or computer). You can export a backup file yourself, and erase everything in Settings › Backup & data.

Location

If you allow it, your location is used on your device to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction. It is never sent to us. On Android and iOS, resolving a city name from GPS coordinates may use the device’s built-in geocoder service; choosing a city from the built-in list stays completely offline.

Internet

The app connects to the internet only for:

  • Recitations: downloaded over HTTPS from hisnmuslim.com and everyayah.com when you choose to download or stream them.
  • Updates (direct-download builds on Android and Windows): checked against GitHub Releases (api.github.com / github.com) after setup, weekly, or when you tap Check now; you can turn automatic checks off in About. Copies installed from Google Play or the App Store are updated by the store and never make update requests.
  • External source links: links to sunnah.com, quran.com or hisnmuslim.com open in your browser when you tap them.

No personal data is sent with any of these requests.

Permissions

  • Location (optional): prayer times and qibla.
  • Notifications: reminders you turn on.
  • Exact alarms: reminders at the exact minute.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional): silence your phone during ṣalāh.
  • Display over other apps (optional): a short dhikr reminder that floats for a few seconds.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
