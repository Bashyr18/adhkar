# Google Play listing: Adhkār

## App name (30 max)
Adhkār: Prayer & Remembrance

## Short description (80 max)
Morning and evening adhkār, prayer times, qibla and duʿāʾ. Private. No ads.

## Full description
Remembrance for every moment, from authentic sources.

ADHKĀR
• Morning, evening, after ṣalāh, before sleep, waking, the night and the adhān
• Arabic from Ḥiṣn al-Muslim and the Qurʾān in the Madani Muṣḥaf style
• Transliteration, translation and the hadith reference for each dhikr
• Listen and learn: optional recitations with a repeat-after-me mode
• Memorise mode hides words step by step until you know it by heart
• Verbatim translations: sunnah.com for adhkār and hadith, The Clear Quran for the Qurʾān

PRAYER
• Accurate prayer times calculated on your phone, worldwide
• Reminders with a "Prayed" button, iqāmah times and qaḍāʾ tracking
• Silence your phone during ṣalāh, then it restores itself
• Qibla compass, with a sun mode that needs no compass
• Travel mode for shortening and combining

DUʿĀʾ
• A private journal of what you are asking for
• Reminders at the authentic times duʿāʾ is answered
• Record what Allah has given

FOR SISTERS
• Break days pause prayer tracking and protect your streak
• Ramaḍān make-up fasts are counted for you

RAMAḌĀN
• Suhūr and ifṭār countdowns, tarāwīḥ and khatm tracker, the last ten nights

HOME SCREEN
• Four widgets: next prayer, adhkār now, dhikr of the day, tracker

PRIVATE
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Everything stays on your phone

## Data safety (answers for the form)
- Does the app collect or share user data? No.
  - Location is used on the device only, to calculate prayer times and the qibla. It is never sent anywhere.
  - Counts, logs, journal entries and settings stay on the device.
- Network use: only to download recitation audio files the user asks for, from hisnmuslim.com and everyayah.com. No user data is sent with these requests.
- Is data encrypted in transit? Not applicable (no user data is transmitted). Audio downloads use HTTPS.
- Can users request deletion? Yes: Settings › Backup & data › Erase all data, or uninstall.

## Permissions to explain if asked
- Location (foreground, optional): prayer times and qibla.
- Notifications: prayer and adhkār reminders.
- Schedule exact alarms (user-granted): reminders at the exact minute of each prayer.
- Do Not Disturb access (optional, user-granted): silence the phone during ṣalāh.
- Display over other apps (optional, user-granted): a short dhikr reminder that floats for a few seconds.
- Internet: optional recitation downloads only.
