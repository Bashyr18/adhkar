Adhkār Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

Adhkār has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, store or share any personal data.

Data on your phone

Your counts, prayer log, journal, settings and location stay on your phone. You can export a backup file yourself, and erase everything in Settings › Backup & data.

Location

If you allow it, your location is used on your phone to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

Internet

The app connects to the internet only when you choose to download recitations. Audio files are downloaded from hisnmuslim.com and everyayah.com over HTTPS. No personal data is sent with these requests. Links to sources open in your browser.

Permissions

  • Location (optional): prayer times and qibla.
  • Notifications: reminders you turn on.
  • Exact alarms: reminders at the exact minute.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional): silence your phone during ṣalāh.
  • Display over other apps (optional): a short dhikr reminder that floats for a few seconds.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
